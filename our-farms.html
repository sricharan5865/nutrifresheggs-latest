Ethical & Humane

Not All Eggs Are Made Equal

It’s a sad reality of today’s egg industry that most egg-laying hens spend their whole lives in cramped wire cages in huge factory factories, which is cruel and inhumane. Commercial eggs from these factories have almost negligible nutritional value and are ridden with chemicals.

SUREGROW FARMS Pvt. Ltd. was started in 2015 to be the change in the agricultural landscape. Our free range hens live in spacious barns with ample space to roam, scratch, roost and exhibit their natural behaviors.

🐔 Only Female Hens & Color Birds

We raise healthy colored heritage breeds that lay thick, natural brown-shelled eggs.

🌱 100% Vegetarian Feed

Natural green forage, herbs, insects, and whole grains. Zero animal byproducts.

Zero Hormones or Antibiotics

Ethical Animal Well-Being

Our hens are never treated with growth hormones, antibiotics, steroids or growth enhancers to artificially increase their weight or egg production. We firmly believe that healthy chickens lay nutritious eggs.

They enjoy large outdoor pastures with plenty of fresh air, sunshine, and natural exercise, significantly reducing stress and resulting in golden-yellow to rich amber yolks loaded with vitamins.
